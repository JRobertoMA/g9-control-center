Configure your Legion G9 controller, without Ultra Control

A lightweight Android app for the Lenovo Legion G9 controllers connected over USB‑C. Dead zones, lights, vibration, back buttons, macros and modes. No accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What it configures

Every change is written to the controller and read back to verify it. Settings are stored in the controller itself, so they work in Android, Xbox and DS5 modes.

  • Sticks & triggers

    Inner and outer dead zones, trigger start and end zones, quick trigger, D‑pad diagonal lock and left stick ↔ D‑pad swap.

  • Vibration & lights

    Grip vibration levels, light ring effect, color, brightness and speed, and the Home button light.

  • Back buttons & macros

    Remap L4, R4, L5 and R5 at once, or record macros of up to 11 keys with press, hold and loop modes.

  • Modes

    Switch to DS5 or Xbox from the app, with Quick Settings tiles and a home screen widget.

  • Profiles & backups

    Named profiles, JSON backups, restore everything and factory defaults.

  • Tablet use

    Optional controller shortcuts and an on‑screen handle for notifications, recents, home and back in full‑screen games.

Supported controllers

G9 Control only sends a controller the commands declared for that model, and never touches a controller in firmware‑update mode.

Install

  1. Download the APK above on your tablet.
  2. Open it and allow installing apps from that source when Android asks.
  3. Connect the controller in Android mode and tap Connect. Android asks for USB permission once.
  4. Later versions can be installed from the app itself: Controller → App updates. It checks the file’s SHA‑256 and signature before installing.

Only download G9 Control from this page or its GitHub repository. Modified copies are being shared; they don’t receive updates and aren’t allowed by the license.

Full guide: user guide · changelog · privacy policy. The app only connects to the internet when you send diagnostics or check for updates.
